# Foreign Trade Marketing and Operations Suite

Version 0.1.0, controlled pilot: twelve roles and fourteen independently authored work skills for search and LinkedIn prospect research, content and email drafts, authorized controlled outreach, and due diligence, documents, sourcing, images and website support. It does not promise exhaustive discovery of every manufacturer.

Install this team through the official DesireCore Market with client 10.0.211+. Review and approve rules and roster through the normal UI, verify the shared work directory and member skills, then configure your own model route and channel accounts. Members default to nested display under the lead; explicit user preferences take priority. Read shared/rules.md. Copy empty shared/templates only on first needed use into private trade-data under the effective workspace; never overwrite existing data or commit workspace records.

Prefer signing in through DesireCore mail management; other explicitly selected trusted connections are supported. Business websites use a local external browser. Reuse authenticated profiles after verifying identity; human users handle login and challenges. LinkedIn, Facebook, TikTok, X, Google Maps/Ads, trade platforms and enterprise customs data require your own accounts, permissions and applicable terms. Some data and services require separate purchase; model and tool usage can also cost money. No subscription, credentials, private records or author-specific model settings are included. Business content can be sent to the configured model and chosen channels. When dependencies are missing, stop the affected calls and provide bounded drafts and concrete setup gaps.

Prior controlled, controller-assisted tests covered LinkedIn research and social actions, Facebook two-account invitation/message/post/comment flows, system-mail round trips, record deduplication and selected normal interruption recovery. Simulated inquiries do not establish commercial results. Facebook company keyword discovery, TikTok/X, licensed enterprise customs, live advertising, hard crashes/concurrency and complete unattended operation remain unverified. New packaging and installation checks are separate; see [release verification](validation/发布验证.md) and the [setup and simulation guide](validation/接入与模拟验证.md). No acceptance claims for third-party noncommercial scripts carry over.

Try the lead with: “Research only: find up to three coffee importers in a country I specify, using at most four queries. Return website, LinkedIn, matching evidence and unknowns; send nothing. Explain what can proceed when an address is missing.”

MIT covers only authored package content. External platforms, models, data, software and assets retain their own terms; see [NOTICE](NOTICE.md).
